### What the owner asked for, and what was built

1. **«لا أريد أي بند يختفي بصمت».** Three filters were deleting rows without a word: a number
   off the quantity column, a row with no quantity, a row with no readable text. On «جدول
   الكميات» that was 2,224 rows found and 1,514 served. Each set-aside row now travels with its
   page, its text and its reason, through the reader, the job and the client; the upload screen
   states the count, breaks it down, and lists the rows on request.

   supplier choices) is written to IndexedDB under the signed-in account and restored at boot.
   Only «ابدأ من جديد» clears it, and signing in as somebody else still does.
3. **«لن أقبل لكل بند أقل من ٥ موردين».** A named material under five sellers is topped up from
   the rest of its family; every borrowed seller carries «على مستوى النشاط» and shows as «مورد
   محتمل». A line whose trade is known but whose material is not now gets the family's sellers
   in its own field. An empty directory says zero and is never padded.

### The reader

- A table ROW is wider than a printed LINE. Row grouping used a fixed 6pt tolerance; the
  hospital booklet prints its wrapped lines 6.5pt apart, so two lines merged and interleaved by
  x. `page_lines` now groups by the page's own measured line spacing and `page_rows` gathers
  those lines into the row band. Hospital booklet: 66 rows instead of 59, names correct.
- One heading, several priced lines: the heading travels with every line of its block and an
  inherited code keeps the column it was printed on. Lines tied to a printed code: 55% -> 100%.
- The heading is no longer «the longest clause» — that is the specification tail. It is the
  first Arabic clause that names something and states no condition. On «جدول الكميات», names
  beginning mid-sentence went from common to zero; 1,001 of 1,514 open with a head or a noun.
- These fonts print the lam-alef ligature backwards, so «بلاط» arrives as «بالط». The repair is
  a lookup built from the catalogue's own 81 lam-alef words, not a guess; 233 of 1,514 names on
  «جدول الكميات» change to the spelling the trade uses.
- Page furniture (titles, column headings repeated on most pages) no longer leaks into the
  first item of every page.

### Matching, speed and honesty

- Saudi booklets open almost every line with «توريد وتركيب واختبار وتشغيل». Those four words
  drag the score below its floor. They are dropped for RESOLUTION only. Across the five test
  booklets, 336 lines: named 6 -> 77, family-only 3 -> 24, unrecognised 327 -> 235.
- When a name resolves to nothing, the line's own description is tried: 20 more lines named.
- `/boq/match` was answering in 15-20 s. Two causes, both measured: the intent lookup parsed six
  evidence fields out of JSON for every row of a 6,700-row intent before discarding all but
  twelve (327ms -> 142ms after ranking first), and two per-LINE regex sweeps ran inside the loop
  over 2,512 catalogue items (21,367ms -> 1,208ms on 200 lines, byte-identical results). A
  batch of forty lines: 15 s -> 3.6 s.
- A stale map row is now skipped rather than refusing its whole material. One old row used to
  make a material serve nothing at all, including the owner's own MANUAL links — which is what
  reduced a 1,514-line booklet to 25 matched suppliers during a rebuild.
