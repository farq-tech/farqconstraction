Sealants

  1. Products
  2. Sealants

For over a century, Sikaflex polyurethane sealants have been the product of choice in many high profile construction projects. With excellent adhesion, dirt resistance, paintability and the ability to be immersed in water after curing without affecting its properties, Sikaflex remains unmatched. Whether you are sealing expansion joints in your sidewalk or windows in your store front, Sika has the perfect sealant for you.

Non-Sag

Sikaflex® + Construction Sealant

Sikaflex® + Mortar Fix

One-component, textured, polyurethane sealant for sealing mortar cracks/joints

Multi-purpose, non-sag, hybrid sealant and adhesive
