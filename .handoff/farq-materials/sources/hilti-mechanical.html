Post-Installed Anchors

Designed to help deliver significant time savings

Whether you are hanging threaded rod overhead for pipes, conduits, trapezes, cable trays or HVAC ducts, Hilti has post-installed anchor options available for your concrete or concrete over metal deck applications. If you're installing anchors after the concrete has been poured, choose from Hilti's selection of screw anchors, drop-in anchors, and wedge anchors for hanging threaded rod.

HDI Drop-in Anchors

Hilti HDI+, HDI-P, HDI-P TZ, and HDI TZ drop-in anchors with automatic setting tool take productivity and reliability to the next level. For rod hanging applications in post-tension slabs, hollow core panels and precast panels, this innovative rod hanging system offers productivity-based advantages to installers with no overhead hammering required. 

The Hilti HDI-P TZ is the top-rated anchor for applications requiring cracked concrete and seismic approval when minimal concrete embedment is allowed. Requiring just 3/4" of concrete embedment, this anchor is ideal for concrete slabs with post-tensioned cable.

Kwik HUS Screw Anchors

Kwik HUS-EZ I

The more overhead threaded rod hangers you need to install, the more you'll come to appreciate the ultimate performance screw anchor with a threaded rod receptacle built into the extended hex-shaped drive head. It works with an American National Standards Institute-tolerance drill bit, so there is no need for special close-tolerance bits. And its coarse thread design means little to no hammering and quick drawdown for significant time savings compared to conventional anchors. All that, plus superior holding, makes the KWIK HUS-EZ I a simple and efficient choice. 

Shop Kwik HUS-EZ I anchors

Kwik HUS-EZ E

Turn to the Hilti KH-EZ E when the application calls for an externally-threaded screw-in anchor to hold rod hangers combine the ease of hammer-free drilling and reduce drilling demandands to deliver significant time savings when compared to conventional anchoring systems. The optimal thread design of the KH-EZ E gives it excellent holding power in a wide range of piping and electrical installations. The KH-EZ is designed specifically for close-to-ceiling applications and does not require special bits for installation, maing it an ideal solution for applications requiring an ICC-ES report for cracked concrete and seismic loading.

Shop Kwik HUS-EZ E anchors

Kwik HUS-EZ PM and PL

Utilize the Hilti KH-EZ PM and KH-EZ PL pan head screw anchors for applications where a washer and anchor are typically needed. Eliminate the need to anchor with multiple components during installation and benefit from the inclusion of an oversized washer head to fasten a multitude of fixtures to concrete or grout-filled CMU with these medium (PM) and large (PL) pan head screws. With the Torx pattern built into the head design for quick and easy installation, the KH-EZ PM and PL screw anchors are manufactured with perceptive design to fit into tight spaces effortlessly.

Shop Kwik HUS-EZ PM and PL anchors

Kwik HUS-EZ

KWIK HUS-EZ offers one solution for every zone: cracked concrete, uncracked concrete, concrete over metal deck and grout-filled CMU blocks in seismic and non-seismic zones.  KWIK HUS-EZ is a reliable, easy-to-set and fully-removable fastening solution with a comprehensive cracked concrete and seismic ICC-ES approved portfolio from 1/4" to 3/4" and an ICC-ES approval pending for grout filled CMU blocks.
